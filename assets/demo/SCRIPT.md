# Demo video script: Orbio Build Week

**Target length:** 90 to 120 seconds (also works cut to 60s)
**Format:** Loom or screen + bubble cam
**Face:** bubble cam. 5 to 8s intro looking at camera, small circle while driving UI, face again on end card. Not a full face takeover.

## Script

### Hook (0 to 8s)
**VO:** “Most AI tools answer. Roberto acts: always on, in a Room you share, powered by one Orbio key.”
**ON SCREEN:** robertoagent.com hero → cut to a live Room.
**CAM:** look at camera for the first line, then glance to screen.

### Setup (8 to 25s)
**VO:** “Create your Roberto. On first run, paste your Orbio API key. We auto select DeepSeek Flash on that key so the welcome meeting and the room brain hit Orbio immediately.”
**ON SCREEN:** onboarding → Orbio key field → model picker showing DeepSeek V4 Flash Latest (Orbio).
**CAM:** bubble only.

### The agent works (25 to 55s)
**VO:** “Hand off real work. Roberto browses, compares, writes, follows up, and pings you when it needs a decision. Guests share the Room; they never pay. Your key and your plan power it.”
**ON SCREEN:** Room chat with a concrete task. Browser viewer open. Agent finishing a result in thread.
**CAM:** bubble only.

### Why Orbio (55 to 75s)
**VO:** “One OpenAI compatible Orbio key unlocks the models and tools. If your balance dips, managed Orbio falls back so the session doesn’t die, and we track spend per user, agent, and Room.”
**ON SCREEN:** Usage / Impact with Orbio attributed usage (no sensitive numbers, no keys).
**CAM:** bubble only.

### Close (75 to 100s)
**VO:** “Always on personal agent. One Orbio key. Built for Orbio Build Week, shipping as a real product at robertoagent.com.”
**ON SCREEN:** logo + URL + “Orbio Build Week · @JussCubs”
**END CARD:**

https://github.com/RobertoAgent/orbio-build-week

**CAM:** look at camera on the last line.
